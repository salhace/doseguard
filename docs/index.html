    How it works
    Same routine. One extra check.
    
      
    Nothing changes for the patient. Take the cap off, inject, put it back. The cap does the rest.
    
    
    
      
    01Cap off: check first
    The moment the cap comes off, it compares the time with the last dose. Inside 3 hours it shows STOP and beeps, before the needle goes in.
    
      
    02Cap back: dose logged
    An injection takes several seconds, so a cap-off of 6 s or more is logged as a dose. A quick peek is ignored.
    
      
    03Watches the insulin
    A thermistor next to the cartridge catches freezing, adds up minutes above 30 °C and counts down the 28-day life of an opened pen.
    
      
    04Keeps family in the loop
    Doses, missed days and alarms go over MQTT to this page. The log is stored in flash and survives power loss.
